Delete your account or data

outtoeat (Eat Out Oz) — last updated 15 October 2026

This page explains how to ask us to delete an outtoeat account and the personal information linked to it. It applies to restaurant accounts created on the outtoeat restaurant portal, and to diners who have made a table booking or contacted us.

How to request deletion

  1. Email enquiries@eatoutoz.com with the subject “Delete my outtoeat account”.
  2. Send it from the email address on the account (so we can confirm it is you), and include the restaurant name if it is a restaurant account.
  3. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your account login and contact details
  • For restaurants: the listing content you submitted (description, photos, menu, specials and links) and your account profile
  • For diners: booking details (name, email address, phone number) linked to your email address

What we may keep

  • Billing and subscription records for restaurants, kept for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax purposes. Card details are held by our payment processor, Stripe, not by us.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that no longer identify you.
  • Security and abuse-prevention logs, kept for a short period.

If you are a restaurant with an active subscription, we will cancel it as part of deleting your account so you are not billed again.

For more about how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy.
